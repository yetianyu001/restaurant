Our story01 / 04

Born of the hearth

One fire.
Every dish.

Ember & Oak started with a simple bet: that a single wood fire, tended well all evening, could carry an entire menu. No gas, no electric ovens — just oak, patience and good instincts.

Seven years later the hearth still burns at the centre of the room. Guests sit close enough to feel the heat, and the kitchen cooks whatever the season and the smokehouse allow.
